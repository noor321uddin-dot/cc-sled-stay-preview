FAQ

Questions before you load up

How close is the riding?

Eagle Pass staging is about 3 minutes away. Revelstoke is 30 minutes east, and all eight zones on the map are within about 30 minutes.

Where does the truck and trailer go?

There's plenty of room in the main lot, and powered parking beside the cabins. It is included in the Sled & Stay rate, and not in the cheaper cabin-only rate.

Can I dry my gear overnight?

Yes, in selected cabins. In the new Selkirk the bunk room converts into a drying room, so pick the Selkirk if your crew needs the space.

How does Early Bird access work?

Tell us where to send your access. It lands in your inbox with the booking link, and you choose your own dates. First come, first served.

What if our plans change?

Cancel 7 or more days before arrival and you are refunded, less a $15 admin fee. Inside 7 days it is 50%, and inside 72 hours there is no refund. Mountain roads and weather change fast, so we recommend travel insurance.
